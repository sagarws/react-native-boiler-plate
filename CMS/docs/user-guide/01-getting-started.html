Installing the app on Windows

TrackVid CMS is a desktop app. You install it once on each computer that will file claims. The installer for a normal Windows PC is named TrackVid-CMS-x64-setup.exe (there is also an ARM64 version for ARM-based Windows laptops; your TrackVid contact will tell you if you need it).

The installer is a "one-click" installer. It does not ask questions or let you pick a folder. It installs for your Windows user only, so you do not need administrator rights. It creates a TrackVid CMS shortcut on the Desktop and in the Start menu, and opens the app when it finishes.

How to install

  1. Download TrackVid-CMS-x64-setup.exe from the link your TrackVid contact gave you.
  2. Double-click the downloaded file.
  3. If Windows shows a blue box saying Windows protected your PC, click More info, then click Run anyway. (See the note below for why this appears.)
  4. Wait a few seconds while it installs. There are no further screens to click through.
  5. The app opens by itself on the sign-in screen. Next time, open it from the TrackVid CMS shortcut on your Desktop or Start menu.
Why does Windows warn me?The installer is not yet digitally signed, so Windows SmartScreen does not recognise it and shows "Windows protected your PC". This is expected for this app. Only choose Run anyway for a file you received from TrackVid. If you downloaded it from anywhere else, do not run it.
TipSome antivirus tools also pause a new, unsigned program the first time it runs. If the installer seems to do nothing, check your antivirus notifications and allow TrackVid CMS.

Only one copy of the app can run at a time. If you double-click the shortcut while it is already open, Windows brings the open window forward instead of starting a second copy.

Signing in for the first time

When the app opens and you are not signed in, you see the sign-in screen. On a wide window the left half shows the TrackVid brand ("Smarter Claims. Stronger eCommerce."); the right half is the form, headed Welcome back — "Sign in to continue to TrackVid CMS."

You sign in with the same email and password you use for your TrackVid account. This is not your marketplace (Myntra, AJIO, Flipkart and so on) login — those are set up later on the Login page.

How to sign in

  1. Type your email address in the Email box (for example, you@company.com).
  2. Type your password in the Password box. Click the eye icon inside the box to show or hide what you typed.
  3. Click Sign in (or press Enter). The button shows a spinner while it checks.
  4. When it succeeds, a green message Signed in successfully appears and the app opens on the Dashboard.

The app version number is shown at the bottom of the sign-in screen (for example Version 0.1.20). Support may ask you for it.

TipYou stay signed in after you close the app. Next time it opens, it shows a short loading spinner and then goes straight to the Dashboard — no need to sign in every morning.

The Responsible Use agreement

The first time someone from your company signs in, a box titled Responsible Use & Agreement appears over the app. It explains that you are responsible for how the app is used, that each marketplace's rules and the law apply, and that TrackVid is not responsible for actions a marketplace takes on an account.

  1. Read the text.
  2. Tick the box I understand and agree to use this application responsibly and in accordance with applicable platform rules and laws. The agree button stays greyed out until you tick it.
  3. Click Agree & Continue.

The answer is saved for your whole company, so you are normally asked only once. Clicking Exit records that you did not agree and closes the app. The box cannot be closed any other way.

NoteIf you see That did not save — … under the checkbox, the agreement could not be sent to TrackVid (usually an internet problem). Fix the connection and click Agree & Continue again. If the app cannot check your company's answer (for example, you are offline), it shows the agreement again to be safe.

How to make the side menu narrower or wider

  1. Click the sidebar icon at the far left of the top bar (its tooltip reads Collapse the navigation).
  2. The menu shrinks to icons only, giving the page more room. Hover over it to see the names again.
  3. Click the same icon (now Expand the navigation) to bring the full menu back.

On a small window the menu hides completely; the same icon (Show the navigation) opens it.

How to sync data from the server

Use Sync when numbers or lists look out of date, for example after someone changed something in TrackVid on another computer.

  1. Click the circular-arrows icon in the top bar. It spins while working.
  2. When it finishes you see a green Synced message.
  3. If you see Synced, but 2 could not be refreshed — check your connection (the number varies), some data could not be downloaded. Check your internet and click Sync again.

The account menu

Click your initials and name at the top-right. The menu shows:

  • Your company name at the top, with the email you signed in with underneath. If you work for more than one company, this tells you which company this window is filing claims for. (Just after signing in, your own name may show for a moment until the company name has loaded.)
  • View profile — opens the User detail page with your name, email, phone and app version.
  • Sign out (in red) — signs you out. See Signing out.

Messages: toasts and the notification centre

Toasts

Short messages ("toasts") pop up at the top-right, just below the top bar, to tell you something has happened. Each one has an icon that tells you its type:

IconMeaning
Green tickSuccess — something finished correctly.
Red alertError — something failed and may need you.
Info markInformation — something started or arrived (for example a claim run started).
  • A toast disappears by itself after about 4 seconds.
  • Hover your mouse over a toast to keep it on screen while you read it.
  • Click the × on a toast to close it straight away.
  • At most three toasts show at once; older ones make way for newer ones.
  • A toast shows only one or two lines. The full text is always kept in the notification centre.

The notification centre

Everything the app has told you since it was opened is kept in the notification centre, so you can read a message you missed.

  1. Click the bell icon in the top bar. The red number clears when you open it.
  2. The panel lists messages, newest first, with a coloured dot (green success, red error, blue information) and how long ago it happened, for example "5 minutes ago".
  3. If a message says click to expand, click it to see the full text, such as the complete error. Click again to collapse it.
  4. Click Clear to empty the list. When it is empty it says Nothing yet.
  5. Click anywhere outside the panel, or press Esc, to close it.
TipWhen you ask support for help with an error, open the bell, expand the message and read out (or screenshot) the full text.
NoteThe notification centre is cleared when you close the app.

The Background work panel

Claims and other automations run in the background, so you can keep using the app while they work. Whenever something is running or has just finished, a panel titled Background work appears in the bottom-right corner of the window. It stays there on every page, so you can always see whether work is still going.

The panel header

The header shows a spinning icon while something is running (or a green tick when everything is done) and a short summary:

  • 2 running · 45% done — two jobs are working, and together they are 45% complete.
  • 3 queued — claims are waiting their turn.
  • 1 finished — nothing is running; results are waiting for you to read.

Header buttons:

  • Click the title, or the arrow button (Hide jobs / Show jobs), to shrink the panel to just its header or open it again.
  • Click the diagonal-arrows button (Show the tally and failed AWBs) to make the panel bigger and see the list of failed AWBs. Click it again (Show less) to return to normal size.

What each row shows

Row titleWhat it is
Ticket creation · Myntra (or another platform)A batch of claims being filed after you pressed Create Ticket. The second line shows the AWB being filed now, or at the end "All 12 tickets created" / "10 created, 2 failed".
Claim · Flipkart (or another platform)A single claim waiting or being filed. Shows its AWB number, and Running or Queued.
NDR/DDR bulk claim · AjioAn AJIO SPF bulk claim run. See 6. SPF bulk claim.

Re-claim · MyntraA Myntra re-claim (dispute) run. Shows "Next in 20s" while it waits between disputes.

Each row can also show:

  • Progress — "8 of 20" and a progress bar (blue while running, green when finished).
  • Tally — successful count, failed count, and how many are remaining.
  • Failed AWBs (in the bigger view) — each failed AWB with the reason. You can select and copy the AWB number.
  • An open-window icon (Reopen this job's window) — brings back the window for that job if you minimised it.
  • An × (Dismiss) — appears only on a finished job, to clear it from the panel. A running job cannot be dismissed.

When nothing is running and nothing is left to read, the panel disappears.

TipFor the full history of past runs, open Background Process in the side menu.

The Automations switch

At the bottom of the side menu is the Automations switch. It is the master on/off switch for everything the app does on a schedule (login updates, report syncs, Refill Data, Status change, Auto claim).

  • Under the switch, a status line reads Idle, Running 1 · 2 queued, Stopping… or Off.
  • Switching it off shows "Automations off — nothing runs in the background until you switch it back on." If something was running, it stops after the step it is on.
  • Switching it on shows "Automations on — catching up on anything that was missed."
  • The eye button beside it (View scheduled automations) shows what is running, queued and scheduled.
  • If a red box Automations paused appears above the switch, the app paused itself because marketplace logins need re-capturing. Click Re-capture on the Login page to fix them.

Full details are on 10. Scheduled automations.

Closing the app while work is running

If you close the window while an automation is still running, the app asks Close TrackVid CMS? and names what is running (for example "Auto claim is still running."). It warns that closing now can stop it part-way, and that a claim being filed might already exist on the marketplace without the app recording it.

ButtonWhat happens
Keep runningThe window stays open and the work continues. This is the safe choice.
View Background ProcessKeeps the app open and takes you to the Background Process page to watch the run.
Quit anywayThe step in progress finishes, nothing new is started, and the app closes.
ImportantAvoid Quit anyway during a claim run. Wait for the Background work panel to show the run has finished, then close the app.

App updates

The app checks for a new version each time it starts and downloads it in the background. You do not need to download anything yourself.

  1. While a new version downloads, the top bar shows a small progress bar and Downloading version 0.1.21 — 40%. Keep working as normal.
  2. When it is ready, the top bar shows Version 0.1.21 is ready to install. "Installing closes and reopens the app."
  3. Click Restart and install to install now. The app closes, installs and reopens by itself within a minute. You stay signed in.
  4. Or click Later to hide the notice. The update is kept and installs automatically the next time you close the app. The notice appears again the next time you open the app if it has not installed yet.
NoteInstalling closes the app, which stops any automation running on this computer. Choose a moment when the Background work panel shows nothing running. Unfinished claims are picked up on the next run.

You can also check for updates yourself from Settings → App version or from your profile page.

The "Update required" screen

Sometimes TrackVid retires an old version. If your version is no longer supported, the app shows a full-window screen titled Update required: "Version 0.1.15 is no longer supported. Version 0.1.21 will install automatically." You cannot use the app (or even sign in) until it is updated. Any release notes are shown in a grey box.

Signing out

Sign out when you hand the computer to someone else, or to sign in as a different user or company.

  1. Click your initials and name at the top-right.
  2. Click Sign out.
  3. The app returns to the sign-in screen.

You can also sign out with the Sign out button on the User detail page. When someone signs in as a user from a different company, the app may show the Responsible Use agreement again for that company.

Light and dark appearance

The app opens in a light appearance. There is no light/dark switch for everyday users: the controls for colours and mode are not shown in normal use. If your company has had a different appearance set up, it applies automatically every time the app opens.
