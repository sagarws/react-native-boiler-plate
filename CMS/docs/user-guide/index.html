TrackVid CMS — User Guide

How to use the TrackVid CMS desktop app to sign in to your marketplaces, file claims one by one or in bulk, and let the app's scheduled automations keep your claims moving.

Who this guide is for Operators who use the installed TrackVid CMS app on Windows or macOS. It covers every screen a normal operator sees. Features that are only visible in developer mode are not included.

A typical day

  1. Open the app and check the Dashboard.
  2. Go to Login and fix any marketplace sign-in shown in red.
  3. File new claims on Create claim, or several at once with Bulk claim.
  4. Review and follow up on existing claims in Manage claims.
  5. Keep the app open: the scheduled automations run on their own, and you can follow them in Background process.
Keep the app running Scheduled automations only run while the app is open, you are signed in, and the Automations switch at the bottom of the side menu is on. If the app was closed at a task's time, the missed run happens once when you next open it.
