Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It shows, for every marketplace your company uses, how many claims there are, how they are going, how much money has been recovered, and what needs your attention today.

On this page
  1. The header bar and Refresh
  2. The six summary tiles
  3. Claim outcomes by platform
  4. Needs attention
  5. The Platforms table
  6. Loading, empty and error messages
  7. A daily routine using the Dashboard

To open it, click Dashboard in the side menu, or click TrackVid Operations in the top bar from any page. The Dashboard only shows information; nothing on it changes your claims. Several tiles and rows are clickable and take you to the page where you can act.

The header bar and Refresh

At the top is a bar reading "Claims, logins and master data for every platform this company runs · updated 5 minutes ago". The "updated" part tells you how fresh the figures are.

How to refresh the Dashboard

  1. Click Refresh at the right of the header bar. The button shows a spinner while it works.
  2. The figures reload and "updated" changes to just now.
TipThe Sync button (circular arrows) in the top bar also refreshes the Dashboard, and at the same time downloads the latest shared data from TrackVid. Use it if Refresh alone does not show a change you expect.

The six summary tiles

Below the header are six tiles. Together they answer "how are we doing?" for the whole company, across all platforms.

TileWhat it showsClick it to…
RecoveredTotal compensation (in ₹) the marketplaces have paid out on your claims. The icon turns green when this is above zero. If it says "Not available yet — the server has to be updated to report it", TrackVid has not started sending this figure; it shows — until then.(not clickable)
ClaimsTotal number of claims, how many platforms they are on, and how many are queued on this machine (waiting to be filed by this computer).Open Manage Claims.
Approval rateApproved claims as a share of claims that have been decided (approved or rejected). Undecided claims are not counted. A green bar fills to the same share. For example "45 approved of 60 decided" gives 75%. If nothing is decided yet it says "No claim decided yet".(not clickable)
In reviewClaims the marketplace is still looking at, and what percentage of all claims that is.(not clickable)
Failed to proceedClaims that could not be filed. When above zero the icon turns amber and the tile says "Could not be filed — open the claim queue". When zero it says "Nothing stuck".Open Manage Claims to see and retry them.
Logins need attentionMarketplace accounts whose saved login has expired, been rejected, or is missing. When above zero the icon is red and says "of 8 accounts — automations on them will fail". When zero the icon is green: "All 8 accounts are signed in".Open the Login page to re-capture them.
Tip"Approved" includes claims marked Paid — a paid claim was approved first.

Claim outcomes by platform

This card compares your platforms side by side. Each row is one platform (with its logo), sorted with the platform that has the most claims at the top.

  • The coloured bar shows how that platform's claims are split by status. Hover your mouse over any part of the bar to see the exact number and percentage, for example "Rejected: 12 (8%)".
  • On the right is the total number of claims, and underneath: "75% of 60 decided" (the approval rate and how many claims it is based on), "None decided yet", or "No claims".
  • Click a row to open Manage Claims.

The colour key (legend) is at the top-right of the card:

ColourStatusMeaning
GreenApprovedThe marketplace accepted the claim (includes Paid).
Amber / brownPendingWaiting for a decision from the marketplace.
BlueIn reviewThe marketplace is looking at it.
RedRejectedThe marketplace turned the claim down.
GreyOtherEvery other status (for example Failed to Proceed). Hover to see which statuses are included.
TipA platform with a lot of red compared with the others is worth a closer look — check the rejection reasons in Manage Claims.

Needs attention

This card is your to-do list. It lists, most urgent first, the problems that stop claims from being filed. Click any line to go to the page where you fix it.

LineIcon colourWhat it meansClicking it opens
"3 logins need updating" — followed by the platforms, for example "Myntra 2 · Ajio"Red keySaved marketplace logins have expired or been refused. Automations on those accounts will fail until you re-capture them.Login
"5 claims failed to proceed" — with the platformsAmber warningThese claims could not be filed and are waiting for you.Manage Claims
"Master data is out of date on 2 platforms" — for example "Ajio 3 days ago · Snapdeal never synced"Grey databaseThe order/return data the app downloads from that marketplace has not been updated for more than 48 hours, or never. Claims may be missing details.Settings (the report sync tasks in the automation schedule)

When there is nothing to do, the card shows a green tick and All clear — "Every login works and nothing is stuck."

How to clear the Needs attention list

  1. Start with the red login line. Click it, and on the Login page re-capture each account it names (see 3. Login).
  2. Next, click the amber failed-claims line and retry or fix those claims in Manage Claims (see 7. Manage claims).
  3. If the grey master-data line appears, click it. On Settings, check the report sync task for that platform: make sure it is switched On, and click Run now if needed (see 10. Scheduled automations). A login that needs updating is often the reason a sync failed, so fix logins first.
  4. Come back to the Dashboard and click Refresh. Fixed items disappear from the list.

A daily routine using the Dashboard

  1. Open the app. It starts on the Dashboard.
  2. Check the "updated" time in the header. If it is old, click Refresh.
  3. Look at Needs attention. If it does not say All clear, work through it from the top (see How to clear the list).
  4. Glance at Failed to proceed and Logins need attention. Both should be zero by the time you finish.
  5. Use Claim outcomes by platform and the Platforms table to spot any platform with unusually many rejections.
  6. Go to Create claim to file the day's new claims.
