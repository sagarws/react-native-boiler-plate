Create claim

File one claim at a time for a returned order — pick the marketplace, choose what went wrong, attach the return video and photos, and save it (or save it and send it to the marketplace straight away).

What this page is for

Open Create claim from the side menu. The page title reads Create SPF Claim. Use it when a return came back damaged, wrong, incomplete or not at all, and you want to raise a claim with the marketplace.

  • Each claim is for one return AWB on one marketplace.
  • The form changes depending on the marketplace you pick. Every marketplace asks for slightly different details and photos — see Marketplace by marketplace.
  • A saved claim appears in Manage Claims.
  • To raise many claims at once from an Increff QC report, use Bulk claim. For AJIO NDR/DDR claims from a spreadsheet, use SPF bulk claim.

The screen at a glance

AreaWhat is in it
Top barThe page title, and the buttons API/Report (AJIO only), the Feed box, Bulk Claim (only if your company has it), Reset, Save and Create Ticket.
Left side (wider)E-commerce Platform, the claim type boxes, any extra boxes the marketplace needs, the account username, the AWB numbers, SKU, remark, and the return and forward videos.
Right side (separated by a thin line)The photos and documents the marketplace needs — for example Return Product Image, Shipping Label, packing or evidence photos, POD proof.

A red star (*) next to a field name means the claim cannot be saved without it. On a narrow window the right side moves below the left side.

Before you start

  • Sign in to the marketplace accounts you file under (see Login). The account boxes on this page list the accounts saved for your company.
  • Pick the right feed. Videos and photos you upload are stored under the feed shown in the Feed box. The app remembers the feed you used last time.
  • Have the return video ready. Every claim needs a return video — either one already recorded in TrackVid (found automatically from the AWB), a file you upload, a recording you make now, or a link you paste.
  • Optional: a camera. With a webcam connected you can take photos and record videos directly from this page.

Choosing the marketplace and the claim type

E-commerce Platform

The E-commerce Platform list shows only the marketplaces your company has set up (each with its logo or a coloured dot). Your company may also have its own platforms (for example a D2C website); these appear with a coloured dot and use the same layout as AJIO.

ImportantChanging the platform on a new claim empties the whole form — AWB, videos and photos — because they belong to a different order. Choose the platform first, then fill in the rest.

How to create a claim (step by step)

This is the general flow. The exact fields differ per marketplace — check its section under Marketplace by marketplace.

  1. Click Create claim in the side menu.
  2. Check the Feed box at the top right shows the correct feed.
  3. In E-commerce Platform, pick the marketplace.
  4. Fill in any extra top boxes the marketplace shows (for example Claim Area or Select Fulfillment Channel).
  5. Choose the claim type (and the second, more exact reason if asked).
  6. Check the account username box. If your company has only one account for this marketplace it is already selected.
  7. Type the Return AWB Number. Wait a moment: the app looks up the return video, the forward AWB and other details and fills what it finds (see What the app fills in for you).
  8. Fill in or check the remaining left-side boxes (Forward AWB, SKU, order numbers, remark).
  9. Make sure the Upload Return Video box has a video or a link.
  10. On the right side, add every photo with a red star. Use Rearrange Images if photos from the video landed in the wrong boxes.
  11. Click Save to keep the claim for later, or Create Ticket to save and file it now.
  12. A green message such as CMS created successfully appears and the form empties, ready for the next claim. If something is missing, the field turns red with a message under it — fix it and click again.

What the app fills in for you

When you type an AWB, the app waits until you stop typing (under a second) and then looks it up. You do not need to press Enter.

  • Return video — if the return was recorded in TrackVid, the video is placed in the return video box and you see Video found. If nothing was recorded you see Video not found; upload a file, record one, or paste a link instead.
  • Photos from the video — screenshots taken from the recorded video are placed in the photo boxes on the right automatically. You can move them with Rearrange Images.
  • Forward AWB, customer order number, SKU — copied from your company's imported order data when available. A box you have already typed in is never overwritten.
  • Account — on AJIO, Meesho, Myntra and Snapdeal the matching seller account is chosen for you when the order data knows it, unless you have already picked one.
  • Marketplace-specific lookups (AJIO return order number, Myntra, Flipkart and Snapdeal report lookups, Meesho order data) are described in each marketplace's section.
TipIf you pick an AWB from a suggestion that shows extra text in brackets, for example 1234567890 (Brand), the app removes the bracket part when saving.

The account username box

Each marketplace form has a box named after it, for example AJIO Account Username, Myntra Account Username, Flipkart Account Username. It decides which of your seller logins the claim is filed under.

  • The list shows the accounts saved for your company for that marketplace. The placeholder reads Select account.
  • If there is only one account, it is selected automatically — and the app uses it when saving even if the box was somehow left empty.
  • An empty list usually means no login has been saved for that marketplace. Add one first (see Login).

Adding photos: upload, capture, view, delete

Every photo box works the same way:

  • Empty box with a camera connected: it is split into two halves — Upload (pick a file from your computer) and Capture (take a photo with your camera).
  • Empty box without a camera: click anywhere on it to pick a file.
  • While uploading: a spinning circle shows in the box.
  • When filled: the photo shows as a preview. Click the round eye button in the middle to see it full size. Click the small red bin in the top-right corner to remove it.

Camera access, capturing photos and recording video

The camera permission window

The first time you open the page without camera access, a window appears:

No Camera DetectedNo webcam was found.Click Skip for now and upload files, or connect a camera and reopen the page.

You can always create a claim without a camera — only the Capture and Record options are missing.

How to capture photos

  1. In a photo box, click Capture. A window opens with the live camera picture. Its title names the box, for example Return Product Image or Side Images.
  2. Hold the item in front of the camera.
  3. Click Capture Screenshot or press the SPACE key.
  4. For a single-photo box, the photo is uploaded and placed in the box.
  5. For product side images, you can take up to 9 photos in a row. The counter (for example 4/9) shows how many. Click a thumbnail to preview it, its remove button to delete it, or Clear to delete them all. Click Save to put them in the grid.
  6. Close the window with the × in the top-right corner when done.

How to record a video

  1. In a video box, click Record. The Record Video window opens with the live camera.
  2. Click Start Recording. A REC sign and a timer appear.
  3. Use Pause and Resume if you need a break (the window shows Paused).
  4. Click Stop. The recording plays back for you to check.
  5. Click Save Video to upload it into the box (you see Uploading…), or Retry to record again. Cancel closes without saving.
NoteIf the window shows a red message such as Unable to access webcam, Camera stream not available or Upload failed, close it, check the camera is not being used by another program, and try again — or upload a file instead.

How to add product side images

  1. In Product Side Images, click Upload. A window opens.
  2. Click the Upload area and choose up to 9 photos at once (Max you can select 9 images). Choosing more shows You can only select up to 9 images.
  3. Click Save. The photos appear in the grid. Or click Capture to take them with the camera (see above).
  4. To add more, use the smaller Upload/Capture tile that stays at the end of the grid while there are fewer than 9.
  5. Hover a photo to view it (eye button) or remove it.
NoteWith fewer than 3 photos, saving stops with Minimum three images required; with more than 9, Maximum nine images allowed. If the PDF cannot be built or uploaded you see Could not upload six-side images PDF — … or Six-side images upload failed; check your internet connection and try again.

Rearrange Images

When photos were filled in automatically (from the recorded video, or carried over while editing), a Rearrange Images button appears above the photo boxes. On some marketplaces the window also opens by itself after the AWB lookup.

  1. Click Rearrange Images. A window opens (titled, for example, Rearrange Product Screenshots, Rearrange Packing Images or Rearrange QC Images).
  2. The coloured dots at the top name each box: green = the first box, blue = the middle boxes, red = the last box. Each filled box shows its number in the same colour.
  3. Drag a photo from Available Images (drag to any slot) onto a box that says Drop here, or drag one box onto another to swap them.
  4. Click Save to apply, Reset to go back to how the window opened, or Cancel to close without changes.

Save, Create Ticket and Reset

Save

Checks the form, uploads the side-image PDF if needed, and saves the claim. On success you see a green message (usually CMS created successfully) and the form empties, keeping the same platform. The claim waits in Manage Claims until it is filed.

Create Ticket

Does everything Save does and then starts filing the claim on the marketplace. It is only shown for marketplaces the app can file automatically, and never while you are editing an existing claim.

ImportantIf you see a blue message such as Claim created. Auto claim not started., the claim is saved — only the automatic filing did not start. Do not enter the claim again; that would file it twice. Start it from Manage Claims instead.

Reset

Empties every field, photo and video on the form. The platform stays the same. Nothing that was already saved is affected.

Editing a claim you already filed

To change a saved claim in full (including its photos), open it from Manage Claims:

  1. In Manage Claims, click the edit (pencil) button on the claim's row. Its tooltip reads Open this claim in the full form. (It is greyed out with This row has no AWB to look the claim up by when the claim has no AWB.)
  2. This page opens titled Update SPF Claim, with a back-arrow button next to the title. The claim's details, videos and photos are loaded, and you see Existing claim loaded — Save will update this claim.
  3. Change what you need. The save button now reads Update Claim. Create Ticket is not shown.
  4. Click Update Claim. You see Claim updated successfully, and the form stays open so you can keep editing.
  5. Click the back arrow to return to Manage Claims.
  • On AJIO and Nykaa the stored side-image PDF shows as a View PDF tile marked Stored. It is kept unless you add new side photos. If you add only 1–2 new photos you see Remove the new images to keep the existing PDF, or add at least 3 new images to replace it.
  • If you change the platform while editing, the AWB, forward AWB, customer order number, remark, videos and the main photos are kept. Marketplace-specific details are cleared, and the old photos are offered in Rearrange Images so you can place them again.
